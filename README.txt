Control your TTLock smart locks from Homey: lock and unlock, see the locked state, battery and gateway signal, get a notification when someone unlocks with a fingerprint, passcode or card, create temporary passcodes from a flow, manage IC cards and eKeys, and change lock settings such as auto-lock time, passage mode, sound and privacy lock. The gateway and the door sensor are added as devices of their own. 45 flow cards are included.

IMPORTANT, READ BEFORE INSTALLING. This app talks to the TTLock cloud, and TTLock only lets you do that with your own developer credentials. Before the app can do anything you need three things.

1. A TTLock gateway (G2, G3 or G4) or a lock with built-in WiFi, within Bluetooth range of the lock, and Remote Unlock switched on for the lock in the TTLock phone app (lock settings, Remote Unlock). Without this the lock cannot be controlled remotely at all and the app reports "Enable Remote Unlock in the TTLock app".

2. A TTLock Open Platform developer account with an approved Application. Register at euopen.ttlock.com (Europe) or open.ttlock.com, create an Application (type Others, for example "Homey"), and wait for approval, which usually takes a few working days. The approved Application gives you a client ID and a client secret. Enter both in this app's settings (Apps, TTLock, Configure) together with your region. The developer account is separate from your normal TTLock app account; the app logs in with your normal TTLock app account (the one that added the lock, as it must be the lock's admin) when you add a device, and your password is never stored, only the resulting token.

3. A callback URL for real-time events. In the TTLock Management Center, open your Application and paste the webhook URL that this app shows in its settings page into the Callback URL field. The URL must be copied exactly, including the part after the question mark, otherwise TTLock's URL test fails. With the callback in place, unlock and lock events, door sensor changes and battery updates arrive within seconds. Without it the app still works, but it only learns about changes at the next poll.

Polling is slow on purpose (15 minutes by default) because the free TTLock developer tier allows 30,000 API calls per month; the settings page shows how many calls the app has used this month. TTLock does not send an event when the lock auto-locks, so the app checks the lock state itself once the auto-lock time has passed. Fingerprints can only be enrolled with the phone app; the app can list and delete them.

This is an unofficial app for personal use, tested with one lock and one G2 gateway, and it is not affiliated with TTLock or Sciener. Lock state is "last known": a lock that is opened by hand with the thumb turn is not reported by TTLock, so do not let a security automation depend on the locked state alone.
